About Us

Built by parents who were tired of the weather deciding the weekend

Khelaghor started with one simple idea: kids need to run, climb and get a little dirty, rain or shine. We built a play zone that's soft enough for a wobbly two-year-old and exciting enough to tire out a ten-year-old — all under one roof, with staff trained to watch every corner.

Every surface is padded, every ball pit is sanitised daily, and every ride is checked each morning before the doors open. Parents get a proper seat and a cup of tea while the kids do the running around.
